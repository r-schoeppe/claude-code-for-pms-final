Score machine

The recent-acceptance score from history.py: a yes adds 0.08, a turn-down or a miss subtracts 0.12, held between 0 and 1. Start at 0.5 and tap outcomes, or scrub through Farlight's real pings.

Play it

0.50

A responder who takes fewer than 60% of their pings drifts to zero: 0.6 × 0.08 equals 0.4 × 0.12. After 12 Aug the overall take rate was 64%, and the four were at about 21%.

How much rank does the score cost?

"Same as" turns the gap into minutes of extra travel, using the proximity weight (0.60 in 4.2, 0.45 in 4.1) over the 45-minute horizon. If the responder is closer than the neighbour by more than this, they are still asked first. This is why the score alone cannot explain being unreachable.

Weaknesses in the evidence itself

    Actions

    Ticks stay in this browser only.

    Stopgap, with a better design. Resetting the four to 0.5 still leaves them about 0.12 below healthy colleagues (about 9 minutes of travel) and the next miss costs 0.12 again. Reset two of the four, keep two as comparison for seven days, and agree the success measure first, for example at least one ping a day each.

    Ranked fixes

    1. Stop treating a miss like a refusal. history.py merges them in record_declined; offer.py already returns them separately.
    2. Let stuck scores recover, and persist them. The 2019 TODO in history.py; scores sit in an in-memory dictionary.
    3. Pilot a 75 to 90 second wait. Pair it with a time-to-accept measure first.
    4. Guarantee a fair share of pings for anyone with none for N days.
    5. Revert the 4.2 weights. Lowest: the simulation shows they barely change the low-ranked group. inferred ranking

    What we still cannot tell

    • unknown Why the four miss 59% of the few pings they get: the shorter wait, the phone not buzzing, or both.
    • unknown Why a neighbour outranks a home responder: no travel times or score components in the data.
    • unknown Severity of the 49 unserved callouts, and whether another route handled them.
    • unknown Anything after 7 Sep. The system date is 15 Oct, so ask for a newer extract.
    • unknown Seasonality: there is no prior-year data. Time-to-accept and "active responders" are not in the database either.
