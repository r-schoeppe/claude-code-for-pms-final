1 · What happened

A small change made a bad week permanent.

On 12 August 2026 Rook shipped release 4.2. It cut the time a ping waits for an answer from 90 to 60 seconds, shifted the ranking toward proximity, and made console filters persist. A missed ping lowers a responder's score by the same amount as a refusal, and a score only rises when they say yes. So when misses jumped, four responders sank to the bottom of the list, stopped being asked, and had no way to earn their way back. Nobody had written a brief, a spec or tests for any of it.

3 · The fix

Let a missed ping fade, and tell people it has.

The hotfix is one release. A missed ping still adds a 0.12 penalty, but the penalty halves every two days, so it is mostly gone after about a week. A responder and their handler each see one plain line about it. The rest waits in a backlog. Try it below: switch between Today, plus the hotfix, and plus the backlog, and watch what changes on each screen.

How we will know it worked
Measure: the four get at least 14% of weekly pings in the second full week after launch (from 1.8%).

Pull back if, on a rolling 7 days, acceptance is under 66.7%, callouts nobody took are over 12.9%, or the slowest 10% take over 82 seconds to reach a responder.

4 · Never again

Your skill checks the brief before anyone ships.

Release 4.2 went out with roadmap one-liners and no measure. review-checklist, the skill you built, reads a brief the way the person who must decide would, and says plainly whether they can decide today. Point it at any brief and it checks five things.
